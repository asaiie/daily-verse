The psalmist speaks from a place of isolation and distress, having nowhere else to turn. Today's news carries the weight of those who find themselves in similar straits—displaced by war, removed to unfamiliar places, trapped in systems that offer no refuge. There is something in the raw honesty of this cry that invites us to acknowledge what we see: the suffering of those with nowhere to go, no voice to plead their case, no portion of safety in the land where they find themselves. To sit with this verse is not to offer easy comfort, but to recognize that such cries are heard, and that bearing witness to them—rather than looking away—may itself be a form of mercy.
