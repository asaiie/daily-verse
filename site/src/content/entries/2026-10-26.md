There is something humbling in watching established authorities—those who seemed unmovable, who had shaped their nations for years—fall to forces that few predicted with certainty. The verse reminds us that outcomes do not always follow the logic we expect, that skill and preparation do not guarantee the result we imagine, and that time itself is a force that works in ways beyond our control or prediction. When we witness the unexpected, we might pause to consider what we thought we knew, what we took for granted, and whether our confidence in the permanence of any earthly order was well-placed. History moves in ways that should make us thoughtful rather than triumphant.
