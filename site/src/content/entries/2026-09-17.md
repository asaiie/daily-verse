The psalmist speaks into a world where the vulnerable cry out and their sighs seem to go unheard—where those with power dismiss or ignore the suffering of those without it. Today's news carries many such sighs: women living in fear, civilians caught in larger conflicts, the voiceless affected by decisions made far from their lives. The promise here is not that suffering will be erased quickly, but that the Lord hears what others do not, and that He counts the cries of the powerless as worthy of His attention. In a moment when vulnerability feels widespread and protection distant, we might ask: who do we listen to? Whose sighs do we acknowledge? To whom are we called to be a voice of safety?
