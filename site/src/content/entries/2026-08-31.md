The psalmist's words come from a place of raw distress—not the comfort of answers, but the act of laying bare what cannot be borne alone. Today's news carries the weight of those who are waiting: families at the foot of mountains where rescuers labor, communities in displacement camps, loved ones searching for bodies in water. The verse does not promise swift rescue or justice restored. Instead, it honors the human need to voice what breaks us, to turn our anguish toward the one who is never too distant to hear it. In lament, we do not minimize suffering or rush past it; we acknowledge it fully, knowing that being heard in our darkest moment is itself a form of mercy.
