When we read of children gasping for breath, families losing their daily bread, and workers poisoned by necessity, we encounter the question posed in Christ's own words: whom do we see in the vulnerable? The passage does not ask what we believe about mercy, only what we do with it—whether we feed, clothe, visit, welcome. Today's news shows us millions whom the structures around them have failed. Perhaps the call is not to grand solutions but to notice, first, that they are there, and to ask ourselves honestly what proximity and care might look like in our own hands, our own communities. The measure is not intention but presence, not sentiment but bread.
