The ancient voice of Lamentations speaks to a world grown numb to suffering—to those who pass by while others bear unbearable weight. Today's news carries the particular anguish of those left behind: families waiting for justice that does not come, soldiers honored in dignified transfer, nations pouring vast treasure into conflict while the poor cannot afford to eat. The verse does not demand answers or assign blame; it simply insists that sorrow be witnessed, that the accumulation of human cost not pass unnoticed. We might ask ourselves: what do we see when we look, and what does it mean to truly behold another's grief?
