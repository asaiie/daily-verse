Jesus spoke these words not as judgment to celebrate, but as an expression of the gravity of harm done to children—the vulnerability of the young demands the fiercest protection. Today's news holds stories of children lost to those who twisted faith into cruelty, alongside communities gripped by uncertainty and fear. The harshness of Christ's words here is not meant to satisfy vengeance but to awaken conscience: the protection of the vulnerable is not negotiable, and the corruption of trust in matters spiritual strikes at something irreplaceable. In witnessing such harm, we are invited to grieve deeply and to recommit ourselves to the kind of faith that shields rather than exploits those who cannot defend themselves.
