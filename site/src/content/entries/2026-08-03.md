Yet the passage from Lamentations was written not in denial of such darkness but from within it, and it names something harder than either despair or false comfort: the stubborn, daily insistence that mercies are new each morning, that faithfulness persists even when intervention does not come when we wish it would. The small images of connection scattered through today's news—a photograph that brings a family reason to hope, an app that opens doors for the legally condemned, elders teaching the young to write by hand—these are not solutions to structural crises, but they are traces of the mercy the psalm insists on, present not as vindication but as grace in the midst of suffering.
