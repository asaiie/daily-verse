The voice in Lamentations calls out not for judgment but for witness—for someone, anyone, to pause and truly see what is unfolding. Today's news carries the texture of that cry: the resumption of hostilities, civilians caught in bombardment, thousands fleeing fire, children dying of hunger, animals found poisoned, systems fracturing under strain. The verse does not demand that we solve everything at once or know whom to blame, but rather that we resist the numbness that comes from scrolling past such suffering. To read these headlines with open eyes, to acknowledge the real sorrow of real people and creatures, to let it matter—this is the beginning of mercy, the posture from which any true response must grow.
