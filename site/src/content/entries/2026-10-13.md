The news today moves between moments of sudden loss and the slower erosions of disaster and illness—reminders that life holds both seasons we cannot choose and sorrows that demand our attention rather than our solutions. There is wisdom in recognizing that some days call not for answers but for acknowledgment: the acknowledgment of grief, of vulnerability, of lives cut short or diminished by forces beyond our control. When words and explanations fail—as they so often do—the ancient rhythm of weeping and mourning remains a form of truth-telling, a way of honoring both the weight of what is lost and the dignity of those who bear it.
