When agreements are signed and talking points declared victorious, the shrapnel still falls. When treaties are drawn, the vulnerability of the ordinary remains—the child in a distant country, the families waiting for justice that may never come, the weight of old debts unpaid through generations. Yet the prophet of lament speaks not of naive optimism but of mercies that persist *in the midst* of a broken world, renewed each day not because suffering ceases but because faithfulness does not. To read the news today is to see both: the fragile human effort toward peace, and the persistent, often invisible cost borne by those without power to negotiate. Both truths deserve our attention and our prayer.
