The familiar words of Matthew's judgment scene are not about grand pronouncements but about presence at the edges—showing up for those without voice or protection. Today's news carries the weight of the vulnerable: those caught between warring powers, those in detention facing removal to countries they do not know, children unable to learn because of disability and circumstance, young lives lost to violence and desperation. The verse does not ask us to solve these crises or to judge those responsible, but to notice them, to ask what small act of seeing and care we might offer. In a day of political campaigns and technological ambitions, the question remains as it always has: who are we visiting, and whom are we turning away?
