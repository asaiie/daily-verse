When institutions designed to carry out judgment falter—when the machinery of punishment jams—we are left in an uncomfortable space between intention and outcome. Today's headlines show us a world where violence finds many forms: state violence, criminal violence, displacement, and harm to those without power to resist. The beatings of those who speak truth, the assault on the vulnerable, the weight of systems that grind on—these are not abstract problems but the lived reality of people who cannot escape them. The verse reminds us that mercy is not weakness or failure, but a blessing that circulates: we receive what we extend. In a day when so much news reveals our systems' capacity to harm, perhaps the invitation is to consider what mercy might look like not as an exception to justice, but as its truest form.
