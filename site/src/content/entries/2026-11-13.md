When systems fail—whether digital infrastructure, transportation, or political cooperation—we feel acutely the truth that we are built for interdependence, not isolation. Today's news carries reminders both tragic and mundane: the sudden absence of tools we rely on, the collision that took lives, the fractures widening between nations and communities. The Preacher knew something we keep learning: isolation compounds every difficulty, while genuine connection is the only reliable safeguard against falling. The question each of us faces is whether we are actively building and maintaining those bonds of mutual aid, or whether we are drifting toward a loneliness that multiplies our vulnerability.
