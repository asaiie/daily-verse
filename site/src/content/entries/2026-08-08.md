The voice of Lamentations speaks not in judgment but in anguish—calling out to those who pass by, asking whether they will truly see the suffering before them. Today's news carries echoes of that ancient cry: children lost to violence, the sick turning to desperation when systems fail them, families displaced and caught between borders, outbreaks spreading without adequate response. The prophet does not explain or justify suffering; he names it, holds it up to witness, and asks the hard question of those who hear. In such a moment, our calling may be simply to behold what is happening, to let ourselves feel the weight of it, and to ask what it means to live as people who have truly seen.
