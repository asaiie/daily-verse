When the infrastructure we depend on breaks down—whether power grids, transportation networks, or the basic privacy we assume in our digital lives—we are reminded of our fragility. The day's headlines carry echoes of sudden loss, displacement, and the discovery that systems we trust can fail without warning or explanation. Yet the psalmist does not deny the difficulty; rather, he speaks to those who have felt the ground shift beneath them, inviting them to a courage that is not the absence of fear but the presence of something steadier. In a world where disruption comes swiftly and systems fail, there is a quieter strength available to those who wait for it—not passively, but with the kind of attentiveness that hope requires.
