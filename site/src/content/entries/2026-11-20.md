The psalmist does not minimize or spiritualize away the complaint—he speaks it plainly, without filter, into the presence of God. Today's headlines remind us that the world contains real trouble: people without power within their own marriages, communities threatened by forces beyond their control, workers pressed by systems indifferent to their need, nature itself in upheaval. The practice of naming what is true, without pretense or false resolution, is itself a form of prayer. To pour out complaint is not to lack faith; it is to refuse the lie that everything is well when it is not.
