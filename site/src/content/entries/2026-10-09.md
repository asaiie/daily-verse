When institutions meant to uphold justice are simultaneously championed and undermined, when accountability frameworks are questioned at the highest levels, and when the enforcement of law itself becomes contested terrain, we face a deeper question about what holds society together. The proverb speaks not merely about corruption but about the difference between rule through stable judgment and rule through the arbitrary will of those in power. Today's news invites us to consider: what is the cost of eroding trust in shared systems, and who bears that burden? In times of institutional strain, perhaps our role is not to declare victors but to lament what is lost and to ask what kind of world we are building for those who come after.
