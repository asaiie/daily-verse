The voice of Lamentations speaks from the rubble of what was broken, calling out to those who pass by unseeing. Today's news carries the weight of accumulating sorrows: soldiers fallen, protesters beaten, families unable to afford bread, regions caught in cycles of retaliation that seem to have no exit. The verse does not demand answers or assign blame; it simply insists that we stop and look—truly look—at what is happening around us. To witness suffering, even when we did not cause it, is itself a form of moral attention. We are invited not to defend or explain away, but to pause and acknowledge the reality of others' pain.
