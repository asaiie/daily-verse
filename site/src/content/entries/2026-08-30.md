The ancient voice of Lamentations asks a piercing question: do we truly see those who suffer? Today's headlines scatter our attention across disputes and novelties, yet somewhere in the margins are those experiencing real anguish—fleeing violence, imprisoned without cause, caught in the wrong place when bullets flew. The verse does not demand we solve everything at once, but rather that we pause long enough to truly look, to let their sorrow register, to resist the easy comfort of turning away. In a noisy day, this is an invitation to bear witness.
